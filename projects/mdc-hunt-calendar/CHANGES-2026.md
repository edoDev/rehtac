# What changed for 2026–27 (vs 2025–26)

**For:** Pevely / Jefferson County hunters, then statewide  
**Verified:** 2026-08-18 against the 2026 Fall Deer & Turkey booklet and MDC newsroom  
**Not a legal document.** Confirm at [mdc.mo.gov](https://mdc.mo.gov) before hunting.

## Bottom line for Jefferson County

MDC simplified the statewide rulebook. **On the ground here, three things actually move:**

1. **The five-day CWD firearms portion is gone.** Last year that was an extra firearms window (Nov 26–30, 2025) in CWD Management Zone counties. Jefferson was in that zone. Those days do not come back.
2. **Early youth firearms moved a week earlier** — Oct 24–25, 2026 — so it does not land on Halloween weekend.
3. **The “CWD Management Zone” label is gone**, but the rules that mattered here are still on: year-round feed/mineral ban, voluntary (not mandatory) CWD sampling this November, no antler-point restriction.

Everything else on this page is real statewide change. Some of it does not change a Jefferson sit.

## Jefferson County — what is different vs last year

| Topic | 2025–26 | 2026–27 | Meaning here |
|---|---|---|---|
| CWD Management Zone | Jefferson was inside the 82-county zone | Zone designation **removed** | No zone banner. County rules remain. |
| CWD firearms portion | **Open** in zone counties, Nov 26–30, 2025 | **Eliminated** | Five extra firearms days are gone. |
| Antler-point restriction | Already off in CWD-zone counties | Off **statewide** (last 18 counties dropped) | **No local change** — Jefferson already had no APR. |
| Mandatory CWD sampling | Not required in Jefferson (St. Louis / St. Charles were typical zone-weekend counties) | Four-year rotation; Jefferson **not** on the Nov 14–15 2026 map | **Still voluntary** this year. Free testing all season. |
| Feed / mineral attractants | Banned in the zone (Jefferson shaded) | Same ban, now described as “counties within 10 miles of a detection” | **Still banned** year-round. Benton, Johnson, and Pettis were newly added to the map — Jefferson was already on it. |
| Firearms antlerless portions | Open (Jefferson) | **Still open**; 4 antlerless permits | Unchanged. |
| Telecheck | 10 p.m. day-of (and before leaving the county) | Same wording, printed clearly: **out of county or 10 p.m., whichever comes first** | Unchanged rule; booklet is explicit. |
| CWD Management Permits | 5 contiguous acres in a Core Area | **20** contiguous acres | Only matters if you use those no-cost extra tags. |
| Post-season targeted removal | Used in some CWD areas | **Stopped**; no current plan to resume | Hunter harvest is now the stated tool. |

**Sources:** 2026 FDT pp.6–7, 17, 25, 64–65, 72; [MDC, 12 Dec 2025](https://mdc.mo.gov/newsroom/mdc-announces-changes-deer-hunting-regulations); [MDC, 17 Aug 2026](https://mdc.mo.gov/newsroom/mdc-changes-regulations-upcoming-deer-season); [MDC CWD page](https://mdc.mo.gov/hunting-trapping/species/deer/chronic-wasting-disease). ## Season dates — last year vs this year

Most windows just slide with the calendar (Saturday openers). The structural change is **early youth** and the **deleted CWD portion**.

| Portion | 2025–26 | 2026–27 | Shift |
|---|---|---|---|
| Archery deer | Sep 15 – Nov 14, 2025 **and** Nov 26, 2025 – Jan 15, 2026 | Sep 15 – Nov 13, 2026 **and** Nov 25, 2026 – Jan 15, 2027 | Calendar slide. Closed during November firearms, as before. |
| Early antlerless | Oct 10–12, 2025 | Oct 9–11, 2026 | Calendar slide. Jefferson open both years. |
| Early youth | **Nov 1–2, 2025** (two weekends before November portion) | **Oct 24–25, 2026** (three weekends before) | **Moved one week earlier** to miss Halloween. |
| November firearms | Nov 15–25, 2025 | Nov 14–24, 2026 | Calendar slide. |
| CWD firearms portion | **Nov 26–30, 2025** (zone counties) | **None** | **Removed.** |
| Late youth | Nov 28–30, 2025 | Nov 27–29, 2026 | Calendar slide. |
| Late antlerless | Dec 6–14, 2025 | Dec 5–13, 2026 | Calendar slide. Jefferson open both years. |
| Alternative methods | Dec 27, 2025 – Jan 6, 2026 | Dec 26, 2026 – Jan 5, 2027 | Calendar slide. |
| Fall firearms turkey | Oct 1–31, 2025 | Oct 1–31, 2026 | Same window. Open-county map is in the booklet (p.22). |

**2026 dates:** FDT p.72 (authoritative).  
**2025–26 dates:** published 2025–26 structure (eRegulations Missouri hunting seasons, updated 13 Oct 2025; matches the Dec 2024 commission set). Early-youth rationale: [MDC, 17 Aug 2026](https://mdc.mo.gov/newsroom/mdc-changes-regulations-upcoming-deer-season).

## Statewide rule changes (MDC’s own list)

From FDT p.72 “What’s New for 2026?” plus the Aug 2026 newsroom recap.

### CWD program

| Change | Why MDC says they did it |
|---|---|
| CWD Management Zone removed (was 6 counties in 2012, **82 in 2025**) | Most of the state is now in or next to a detection. A zone map was adding complexity without adding much. |
| CWD firearms portion removed | Five extra days did not move harvest much; hunters wanted fewer portions. |
| Mandatory sampling → **4-year county rotation** (28 counties on Nov 14–15, 2026) | Sample every county over time; simpler than “are you in the zone?” |
| Feed/mineral ban **continues** within 10 miles of a detection | Unchanged policy. New counties on the 2026 map include Benton, Johnson, Pettis. |
| CWD Management Permits: **5 acres → 20** contiguous in a Core Area | Same acreage as other no-cost landowner deer permits. Application mailed to qualifiers. |
| No post-season targeted removals | Public pushback. Hunter harvest is the stated replacement. |

### Harvest rules

| Change | Detail |
|---|---|
| APR gone in the last 18 counties | Now **zero** APR counties. Already gone in former CWD-zone counties (including Jefferson). Yearling bucks travel far; MDC wants them harvestable where CWD is a risk. |
| Nonresident antlered limit **2 → 1** | Regular Nonresident Archer’s and/or Firearms Any-Deer permits. Qualifying **nonresident landowners stay at 2** on their land. MDC cites ~50% more nonresident deer hunters in 10 years and resident access complaints. |
| Permit prices up | Printed on FDT pp.16–19. Resident firearms any-deer 16+ is **$19.50**; nonresident **$360**. We do not have last year’s price table in this folder, so no dollar-to-dollar delta here. |
| Managed hunts / some CA rules | Added, dropped, or rewritten. FDT pp.32–40 and 42–55. Check the area you actually hunt. |

## What did **not** change (easy to miss)

- Legal shooting light: ½ hour before sunrise to ½ hour after sunset.
- Resident antlered limit: two for the year; only one during all firearms portions combined; only one archery antlered deer before Nov. 14.
- Hunter orange still required for all firearms deer portions.
- Jefferson still **open** for both antlerless portions, **4** firearms antlerless permits.
- Baiting is still illegal statewide; the CWD feed/mineral rule is extra and still on here.
- Bear season (BMZ 2, Oct 17–30) is **not** in the FDT booklet. Unchanged source: 3 CSR 10-7.900 / MDC bear pages.

## How to read this in the woods

If you only remember three things for Pevely:

1. **No extra CWD firearms week.** Plan November 14–24 and the later portions. Do not show up the Wednesday after Thanksgiving expecting a CWD-portion opener.
2. **Do not put out corn, minerals, or salt** to pull deer. House-side exceptions are in the booklet (p.6).
3. **You do not have to hit a mandatory CWD station** for a Jefferson deer this year. Testing is still free and still a good idea.
